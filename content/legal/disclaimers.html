Non-ACA and supplemental products

Some products discussed on this site, such as short-term health insurance, fixed indemnity, accident, critical illness, hospital indemnity, dental and vision plans, are not ACA-compliant major medical coverage. They may exclude pre-existing conditions, may not cover essential health benefits, and aren’t a substitute for comprehensive health insurance. Supplemental policies are designed to add to, not replace, major medical coverage.
